# BSD 311 presentation and live demo guide

Target: **15 minutes**, followed by 5 minutes of questions. Divide speaking time roughly equally among all members and keep a short screen recording as backup.

## Suggested timing

1. **Problem and users — 1:30.** Introduce the communication barrier and the four user groups: non-verbal children, parents, teachers, and speech therapists. Explain why an interface that is simple for a child can still be difficult for adults to personalize.
2. **User research findings — 2:00.** Present only findings collected from real participants. Show the two methods used, participant counts, and the strongest evidence that influenced the design.
3. **Personas, scenarios, and HTA — 1:30.** Show two evidence-based personas and the two key task analyses. Connect each task to a measurable usability goal.
4. **Design progression — 1:30.** Show `docs/low-fidelity-wireframes.svg`, then the current prototype. Call out changes in hierarchy, tile spacing, error prevention, feedback, and accessibility.
5. **Live prototype demonstration — 4:00.** Follow the five flows below.
6. **Usability evaluation — 2:30.** Report the test plan, five or more real target users, task success, time, errors, SUS result, and problems ranked by severity. Show two genuine before/after improvements.
7. **Reflection and conclusion — 1:00.** State what worked, the main limitation, and the next development priority.
8. **Questions — after the 15 minutes.** Every member should be ready to explain any stage.

## Live demonstration flows

1. **Sam asks for water.** Choose **Open Sam's board**. On Home, tap **I**, **want**, and **water**, then select **Speak**.
2. **Sam asks for immediate help.** Clear the message, tap **help**, and select **Speak**. Point out that Help, Stop, and No stay in the core area.
3. **A parent adds “My bottle.”** Open **Grown-up area**, press and hold the gate, enter demo PIN `2468`, choose **Add a picture**, enter `My bottle`, upload or take a familiar photo, choose Home only, save, and preview Home.
4. **A therapist reduces visual load.** Switch to Therapist, open **Board layout**, choose **Big pictures**, and show that core word order remains stable.
5. **A teacher adds a classroom word.** Switch to Teacher, add a word such as `quiet corner` to School only, then compare School and Home previews.

Before participant testing, open **Usability study**, use an anonymous code such as `P01`, select the standardized task, and start recording. At the end, mark the outcome and export the CSV. Do not demonstrate invented results in the presentation; show only real, consented sessions.

If browser speech is muted or unavailable, the visual message still works and the interface explains the limitation. Keep a pre-recorded demo as required by the project description.
